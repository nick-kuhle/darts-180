## 1. Competitive reality

Darts 180 is not the first phone/computer-vision darts scorer. The opportunity is a phone-first,
privacy-respecting, confirmable product with a great standalone game platform—not a false claim of
being the only automatic system.

## 3. Positioning hypothesis

### Do not claim

- “The first phone camera darts scorer.”
- “Any camera angle, perfect accuracy.”
- “DartCounter integration” without a signed/sanctioned agreement.
- “Tournament-grade” or “99% accurate” absent published, device-diverse evidence.

### Build toward

- **Most trustworthy phone-first referee:** show uncertainty and retain player control.
- **Best local game experience:** works even with no camera, account, or Wi-Fi.
- **Privacy-respecting CV:** on-device first, transparent opt-in data contribution.
- **Open product platform:** documented event/device protocol, rather than captive hardware lock-in.
- **Affordable path to reliability:** good mount → optional second device → future accessory only if
  hard data shows the need.
